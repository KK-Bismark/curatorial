Product Description

The Sculptural Blazer is a masterclass in architectural tailoring. Designed to bridge the gap between classic menswear structure and contemporary fluidity, it features a unique pleated waistline that creates a dramatic yet effortless silhouette.

Featuring a peak lapel, functional surgeon's cuffs, and our signature tonal lining. This piece is intended to be the cornerstone of the modern professional wardrobe, transitioning seamlessly from morning briefings to evening gallery openings.
